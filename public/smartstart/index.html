SmartStart Simulator

New accounts earn sending privileges through evidence over their first 90 days.

Day 0

A working model of a new-sender trust framework I designed for an email platform. Every new account gets a risk score at signup, starts in a tier with a matching daily sending limit, and moves up or down based on how it actually sends. Press Play to watch seven fictional accounts, from a healthy startup to a bust-out fraud ring, play out their first 90 days. Click any account to see why each decision was made.

Shadow mode is on. SmartStart still classifies accounts and logs decisions, but limits are not enforced. Held volume is projected.
